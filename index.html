So... What Even *Is* Boba?

Boba (aka bubble tea) is a magical drink from Taiwan that combines tea, milk, and chewy tapioca pearls. It's sweet, it's fun, and yes — it's totally Instagram-worthy.

Top 3 Flavors You Gotta Try

  • Classic Milk Tea – The OG. Smooth, sweet, and comforting.
  • Passion Fruit Tea – Tangy, tropical, and super refreshing. Like a vacation in a cup.
  • Mango Milk Tea – Creamy, fruity, and lowkey addictive. Mango lovers, this one’s for you.

FAQ (aka Frequently Asked Boba Questions)

Q: Are the pearls edible?
A: Totally! They're made from tapioca starch and are chewy like gummy bears.

Q: Is boba healthy?
A: Let’s just say... it’s a treat. Enjoy in moderation and you’re golden.
